\section{Introduction}

Reusability is one of the main principles of heterogeneity-aware Knowledge Graph (KG) development \cite{p4,p6}, as defined by the iTelos methodology \cite{p1,p2,p3,p5}. The project documentation contributes to this reusability: a clear description of the resources used and produced, of the process followed, and of the evaluation carried out at each step allows external readers to understand the project and to reuse its outcomes.

\noindent This project builds a KG that supports the interpretation of spatial transcriptomics results. Spatial transcriptomics measures gene expression while preserving the spatial organization of a tissue, and its results are often summarized as biological pathways that score differently across tissue regions, for example on the two sides of a \emph{tissue frontier}, the zone where two cellular regions meet. To read such results, a researcher needs to know which genes make up a pathway, which pathways a gene belongs to, and which cell types are described by marker genes that overlap with a pathway. This information is spread across heterogeneous sources: gene-set collections (MSigDB Hallmark \cite{liberzon2015molecular}), cell-type marker databases (CellMarker 3.0 \cite{hu2026cellmarker}), and the gene nomenclature authority (HGNC \cite{seal2026genenames}). Each source uses its own formats, identifiers, and naming conventions. Integrating them is therefore a typical case of the data heterogeneity that iTelos addresses.

\noindent iTelos follows a \emph{stratified} approach: the resources are handled separately at the language, knowledge, and data layers, and the KG is built through a sequence of phases driven by an explicit purpose. This report follows those phases. It is written incrementally: the sections describing a phase are completed only once that phase has been carried out.

\noindent The report is structured as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Section 2: the project design, i.e. the domain of interest and the informal purpose.
    \item Sections 3, 4, 5, 6: the iTelos phases (Purpose, Language, Knowledge, and Entity Definition) and their activities, divided into knowledge-layer and data-layer activities, with a qualitative evaluation of the resources produced with respect to the purpose.
    \item Sections 7 and 8: the quantitative evaluation of the KG and the metadata produced for the resources handled and generated during the process.
    \item Section 9: conclusions and open issues.
    \item Appendix A: the questionnaire on the use of Large Language Models (LLMs) in each phase; Appendix B: technical material (queries, schema snippets, execution commands).
\end{itemize}
